# Oheo

A bilingual blog with posts, moments, and localized content.

## Language

**Content slug**:
A content item's locale-independent identity, given explicitly or derived from its source path. Post routes and scoped Markdown anchors use this same identity.

**Outline**:
A post's heading navigation, positioned beside or inside the reading area according to available space.

**Theme preference**:
A reader's choice of light, dark, or following the system. The effective theme is light or dark after that preference is resolved.
